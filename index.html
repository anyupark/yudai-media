A letter from the founder

Dear Founder,

Business is about one thing: solving problems.

You solve one for your customers every time they buy. The hard part is everything standing between you and that sale.

I've spent years in the arena with e-commerce brands, and I keep seeing the same pattern. Most problems show up in two places: the creative and the funnel. The ad that no longer stops the scroll. The page that loses buyers on the way to checkout.

But not always. Sometimes it's the offer. Sometimes it's the product itself. Sometimes it's something nobody has looked at yet.

That's why I don't want to be another agency that runs your ads and sends a report on Friday. I want to be your partner. Someone who looks at the whole business with you, finds the real obstacle and stays until it's solved. Then the next one, because there is always a next one.

Even Hercules didn't slay the Hydra alone. Every time he cut off a head, Iolaus was there with the torch, so it could never grow back.

That's the role I want to play for you. Whatever stands in our way, we solve it together.

One last thing, about the name.

εὐδαιμονία Eudaimonia · yoo-die-mo-NEE-a

Yudai comes from the Greek eudaimonia. Eu means good, daimon means spirit. Together they mean human flourishing. Aristotle called it the highest good a life can reach.

I believe business follows the same law as life: as within, so without. When you flourish, your brand flourishes, and the numbers follow. That's what we're really here to build.

We work on a monthly retainer plus a performance bonus, so we earn more when you grow. The exact numbers depend on your brand and goals, and we set them together after the strategy call.

Who is this for?

E-commerce brands that already sell online and want to grow with Meta ads, stronger creative and better retention.

Do I need my own content?

No. We film, shoot and design everything. And if you already have your own production or in-house team, you don't have to make content with us: we write the creative briefs (hooks, angles, scripts and shot lists), your team shoots, and we run the ads.

It's 60 minutes and it's free. We look at your store, ads and tracking with you, show you the biggest leak and the fastest win, and you leave with a plan. No hard pitch.

Because the best ads come from the people who see the numbers every day. When the team that films is the team that runs the ads, every result shapes the next creative, and nobody can blame the other side.
